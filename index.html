Mastering my AI workflow

I now build faster by adapting AI into my workflow right from the inception stage. Tools like ChatGPT and Gemini help a lot with ideation and market research. The next step involves creating faster prototypes using Figma MCP and Copilot to create live-code prototypes that serve as a source of truth for engineering (real-time data handling, UI state transitions, complex interaction patterns) which ultimately leads to faster development. This involves the entire triad - product, design, and development - working faster together towards a problem statement.

I also introduced this workflow for the whole design team to maintain a more consistent style, change how we work, and improve overall efficiency. The model is currently being considered for adoption by other LOBs as well.

Design system for AI
