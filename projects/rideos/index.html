How teams usually start

You do not need to roll out everything on day one.

Most teams begin with one city, one route type, or one part of the business. Get the basics right, let people use it, then expand from there.

01

Pick the first service to launch

Airport pickup, hotel transfer, local cab booking, or staff shuttle. Start with one clear use case.

02

Put your brand and rules on it

Add your name, fares, support number, vehicles, and the details riders should actually see.

03

Bring in the team

Add drivers, vehicles, dispatch notes, and the handful of things your operation needs to function every day.

04

Go live and learn from real trips

Once the first version is working well, expand to more routes, more operators, or more regions.

FAQ

Common questions from operators.

Can this be branded for our company?

Yes. The booking flow, trip page, and ops view can all carry your name, colors, support details, fares, and service setup.

Do we need to adopt expensive infrastructure on day one?

No. Start with a simpler setup first. If the operation grows, the underlying services can grow with it.

Who is this best suited for right now?

Airport taxi operators, EV fleets, hotel transfer teams, concierge transport desks, local city fleets, and branded shuttle services.

Can we request custom flows for our operation?

Yes. If your team handles bookings, vehicles, support, or dispatch in a specific way, that can be shaped around your operation.
